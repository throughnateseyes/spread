Spread — logo concepts

Three directions for the Spread mark. Each is shown as the macOS app icon (1024 / 128 / 32 px), as the 16px mark in the app's top bar, and as a wordmark lockup (Inter SemiBold, outlined), on light and dark. The chosen direction is documented in README.md.

A Seam

Two slides side by side; one photo crosses the seam and keeps going. The gutter cuts the disc but never breaks it, which is exactly what a seamless carousel does.

B Open spread

A magazine lying open: two pages meet at the spine and a full-bleed image runs across both. The print idea of a spread, drawn flat.

C Triptych

Three slides in a row with one sun rising across both seams. Reads as a carousel strip; the picture only makes sense when the slides sit together.

Small-size proof

Rasterized with rsvg-convert and zoomed with nearest-neighbour so the real pixels show. Per concept, left to right: the 32px icon (4x), the 64px icon i.e. 32pt on Retina (2x), and the 16px in-app mark at 1x (8x). Top row light, bottom row dark.

Verdict: A, Seam. It is the only one that stays a clear, ownable silhouette at 16 and 32px (two coloured halves, one white disc). B reads as "book" or "box" before it reads as "spread", and C turns into a bar chart below 64px. A also grows straight out of the blue/purple split square the app already uses, so the brand changes without the product feeling unfamiliar.
